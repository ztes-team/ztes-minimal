# Governed Goldbach convolution

The catalog accepts a finite horizon of 20. Independent left and right lineages publish their complete eligible lists of primes. A query requests the number of ordered pairs for a total and specifies whether eligible input spectra can be reused. Native spectrum and product proposals use a radix-2 number-theoretic transform of length 64 over the field modulo 257. Their criteria check polynomial evaluations and integer pair counts independently. The answer cites the product and lists every ordered witness for the requested total.

An admitted answer containing a pair with a right addend above 13 enables the predeclared change. Its previously active authority checks that evidence and the proposed successor specifications. Admission installs both the right ontology and criterion at version 2. Once the right lineage publishes its new list, the next product can reuse a compatible left spectrum and must use a spectrum of the new right list. The immutable source reference, operand side and transform parameters determine compatibility.

## Expected end

The default agenda admits genesis and the sixteen initial specifications. The [experiment driver](../README.md) supplies the boundary catalog and queries and schedules the controls. For total 20, the initial answer carries the four ordered pairs (3,17), (7,13), (13,7) and (17,3). After activation the right cap removes (3,17). Replay on the earlier prefix still accepts the four-witness certificate.
