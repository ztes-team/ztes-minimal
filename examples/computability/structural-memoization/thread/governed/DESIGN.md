# One thread: reuse, obstruction, decision, governed change, compensation

The series of `regions` runs first under the rules r1. X owes 110 and the first two tiers can pay 100, so a certificate is admitted (Proposition 14). Three fees are debited from the parties of X. The review board, the authority named in the contract, decides on the certificate. The mandate, judged by the criterion in force (R1), then changes the rules to r2: a reserve tier may pay, and plan, settle and certificate v2 are carried by the event. X is derived again and settled 60 + 40 + 10 (Corollary 9). No pattern of r1 is used after the change without a new proof: F revalidates P1 under r2 with one proof, and the catalogue publishes the result as P4. G then reuses P4 and H reuses the pattern of X. Two refunds link the affected parties to their debits, and the closure needs both (§9.3). [Design of the thread](../DESIGN.md)
