# Governed settlement and compensation

A fixed primary cap leaves the beneficiary's recognized loss uncovered. A mandate judged under prior authority activates a case-specific replacement criterion and ontology. A recorded guarantee permits full payment. Separate refund events link each affected participant to earlier debits, and the closure criterion requires complete coverage.

The experiment driver supplies the mandate, evidence and boundary inputs for the waiting, fixed-regime, partially compensated and completed records. From the repository root, run `python examples/computability/structural-memoization/experiment.py --pairs 1 --series-pairs 1` for the complete sequence.
