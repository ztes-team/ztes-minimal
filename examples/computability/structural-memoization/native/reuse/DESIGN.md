# Dispute diagnosis and plan reuse

The transport and cold-chain diagnoses require different evidence. Each refers to its admitted funding summary. The catalogue publishes a verified graph derived in the source case, and the target reads a declared snapshot before proposing a payment under its own policy.

This manifest registers the criteria and ontologies. The experiment driver supplies the observations and schedules the two cases to control the visibility of the publication. It also supplies the exclusion and incompatible-version controls. From the repository root, run `python examples/computability/structural-memoization/experiment.py --pairs 1 --series-pairs 1` for a short complete run.
